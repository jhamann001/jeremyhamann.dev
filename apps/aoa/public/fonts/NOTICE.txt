Total six original files: 446084 bytes before HTTP encoding. They are included
in the source tree and emitted as fingerprinted same-origin assets by Next.
No live Google Fonts request is needed for building or rendering these fonts.
Full Heebo and IBM glyph coverage increases the first-load payload versus the
prior Latin-only preloads; fonts are cacheable and unchanged on later visits.
Next localFont calculates temporary fallback metrics from the bundled files;
these can differ slightly from next/font/google's precomputed metrics.
